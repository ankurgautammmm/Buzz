# 3D Human Anatomy Assets Attribution & Licensing Register
BuzzingBrain Educational Platform — Interactive 3D Human Anatomy Lab

The 3D anatomical models integrated in BuzzingBrain are derived from recognized, peer-reviewed open-science initiatives under Creative Commons licenses.

---

## 1. Human Reference Atlas (HRA) / HuBMAP Consortium
- **Organ Models Included**:
  - `heart.glb` (Adult Human Male Heart — 3D Reference Organ v1.3)
  - `kidney.glb` (Adult Human Left Kidney — 3D Reference Organ v1.3)
  - `pancreas.glb` (Adult Human Pancreas — 3D Reference Organ v1.3)
  - `spleen.glb` (Adult Human Spleen — 3D Reference Organ v1.3)
- **Authors & Contributors**:
  Kristen Browne, Heidi Schlehlein, Bruce W. Herr II, Ellen M. Quardokus, Katy Börner (Cyberinfrastructure for Network Science Center, Indiana University).
- **Institution**: National Institutes of Health (NIH) Human Biomolecular Atlas Program (HuBMAP).
- **Digital Object Identifiers (DOIs)**:
  - Heart: https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM352.BTSQ.586
  - Kidney: https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM698.XJTK.948
- **Source Repository**: https://humanatlas.io/ & https://lod.humanatlas.io/
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- **Adaptations & Transformations**:
  - Axis aligned from biological coordinate system to web GLTF standard (Y-up, centered at origin).
  - Material properties mapped to realistic anatomical PBR shaders (subsurface scattering approximations, diffuse roughness, physiological vascular coloration).
  - Maintained 100% of individual mesh segmentations (chambers, valves, papillary muscles, septum, cortex, medulla, pyramids, hilum).

## 3. Textbook Verification Sources
All anatomical nomenclature, physiological functions, blood supply, innervations, and clinical notes are verified against recognized medical and curriculum authorities:
1. **NCERT Biology (Class 10 & Class 11)** — National Council of Educational Research and Training, India.
   - Class 10 Science: Chapter 6 (Life Processes — Transportation & Excretion)
   - Class 11 Biology: Chapter 18 (Body Fluids and Circulation) & Chapter 19 (Excretory Products and their Elimination)
2. **OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e** — J. Gordon Betts et al., OpenStax, Rice University.
   - Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
   - Chapter 19: The Cardiovascular System: The Heart
   - Chapter 25: The Urinary System
3. **Gray's Anatomy for Students (4th Edition)** — Richard Drake, A. Wayne Vogl, Adam W. M. Mitchell. Elsevier. (Referenced for anatomical spatial orientations and coronary vascular branching verification).
